### 5. Critical Issues Identified

#### A. Brand Query CTR Crisis
"convertr" at position 2.9 should yield 15-20% CTR for a brand term. Currently at 3.7% — **4-5x below expected**. Root cause: The root `/` page captures most brand impressions (802) but multiple locale pages are cannibalizing (pt: 176 impressions, en/video-converter: 91, en: 50, de: 20, etc.).

#### B. Missing OG/Twitter Meta Tags
The base layout `public.blade.php` has no Open Graph or Twitter Card tags. This means:
- Zero rich previews when shared on social platforms
- Missing potential SERP enhancements
- Lower social sharing virality

#### C. No Structured Data
Zero JSON-LD markup anywhere in the codebase. Missing opportunities:
- WebApplication schema → software/tool rich results
- Organization schema → brand knowledge panel
- HowTo schema → featured snippets for "how to convert" queries
- FAQPage schema → FAQ rich results

#### D. Single Encode Page
Only `/encode/av1-to-h264` exists as a dedicated codec conversion page. The route is hardcoded — there's no dynamic route for other codecs. Given this single page drives 484 organic clicks (67% of all organic), replicating to even 3-4 more codec pairs could triple organic traffic.

#### E. Massive Missing Keyword Coverage
The site has zero impressions for mainstream conversion queries:
- "mp4 to mp3" (estimated 100K+ monthly searches globally)
- "heic to jpg" (estimated 50K+ monthly searches)
- "webm to mp4", "mov to mp4", "png to jpg" etc.

No dedicated landing pages exist for these conversions.

### 7. Codebase Assessment

- **Framework:** Laravel with Blade templating
- **Localization:** mcamara/laravel-localization — 25+ supported locales
- **Sitemap:** Spatie laravel-sitemap (exists at /sitemap.xml)
- **Meta tags:** Basic title + description via `@yield` — no OG, no Twitter, no JSON-LD
- **Routes:** Converter category pages + 1 encode page + blog — all auto-prefixed with locale
- **robots.txt:** Allows all crawlers

### 8. Priorities Summary

1. **Highest Impact:** Create more encode/codec pages (replicate AV1 success) — could 3-4x organic traffic
2. **Quick Win:** Fix brand "convertr" CTR — optimize meta titles/descriptions across localized homepages
3. **Foundation:** Add OG tags + JSON-LD structured data — enables rich results and social sharing
4. **Long-term:** Build format-to-format landing pages for high-volume queries — the primary organic growth lever
5. **Monitor:** Traffic declining ~10% MoM — investigate if seasonal or loader.to referral changes
